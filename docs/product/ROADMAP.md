# Product roadmap

## Status
The owner's discovery answers supersede the initial recommendation to launch two games and postpone solo play. All six games and all three product modes are first-release scope. Recommendations and future ideas remain distinct from decisions.

## First playable release
### Confirmed scope
- Dice, Darts, Basketball, Football, Bowling, and Slots, each supporting all three modes.
- Equally important duels, tournaments, and solo play.
- Manual native game emoji attempts with a one-minute deadline; missing it causes match defeat. Tournament matches remain sequential.
- Target 3-5 minutes per match.
- Selectable fixed five/seven-round series or until-victory format; extra paired rounds resolve a tied series without an attempt limit.
- Stakes and random outcomes; no mandatory skill or strategy controls.
- Separate PvP and solo balances and rankings; wealth is the primary success measure.
- Daily grants, no free games, no transfers, no stake caps.
- Four/eight-player single elimination, a third-place match, prizes for prize places.
- Acceptance/joining supplies all consent; no repeated readiness checks. A missed one-minute attempt deadline causes technical defeat and advances the event.
- Spectator visibility, reactions, and playful taunts; no spectator bets.

**Owner delegation:** calculate the economy. ECONOMY.md now supplies starting grants, daily recovery, stakes, prize shares, solo payout tables, and expected-return calculations. Sports outcome mappings and uniform probabilities are explicit assumptions to verify before paying out real game results.

### Agree on the trial rulebook
1. Series interpretation: fixed five/seven rounds and first decisive pair for until-victory, as documented in GAMES.md. Confirm that the latter matches the intended meaning.
2. Verify every native animation mapping and non-seven slot symbol label. Treat probability assumptions as assumptions, not certified odds.
3. Technical-defeat causes beyond explicit withdrawal; double forfeits and cup-wide equal pool division.
4. Trial economy is specified: 1,000 per track, eligible daily help up to 100 below wealth 300, minimum 10, suggested stake 50 and entry 100, prizes 50/30/20. Creator chooses the actual entry fee.
5. Trial ranking uses current funds plus committed stakes; grants reset daily in Europe/Moscow and missed grants do not accumulate.
6. Solo-result visibility and acceptable group message volume remain open.

### Test before expansion
**Recommendation:** small sessions covering the confirmed catalogue, then four-player and eight-player cups. This is a testing sequence, not a reduction of first-release scope.

Observe duration, tied attempts, waiting entrants, bronze participation, technical losses, all-in bankruptcies, dependence on grants, wealth concentration, solo payout inflation, and unwanted messages.

Four-player cups require four matches including bronze: about 12-20 minutes at target pace. Eight-player cups require eight: about 24-40 minutes before transitions/ties. Unlimited tiebreaks prevent a guaranteed finish.

**Open question:** the owner has not chosen success criteria.
**Recommendation:** look for voluntary rematches and return attendance, understood rules, and spectators following the final. Reward collection alone does not demonstrate enjoyment.

## Social expansion
Recurring cups, themed titles, champion status, richer game-specific taunts, and rematch history.
**Ideas:** streak records and revenge labels. Currency bounties and underdog bonuses are not approved. Random play does not justify skill-based underdog odds. Secondary records should not replace wealth as the main progression.

## Future spectator betting
Outside the first release. If desired later, begin with one match-winner pool and agree on currency track, cutoff, void matches, own-match bets, collusion, and settlement. Nonmonetary predictions remain optional.

## Long-term wealth competition
Seasons, historical wealth records, achievements, and a Grand Final are future ideas. Newcomer fairness, qualifying rules, and any balance reset require explicit decisions. No seasonal reset is approved.

## Advanced events and solo expansion
Sixteen entrants, teams, mixed-game cups, jackpots, and themed events are optional later stages. Four/eight-player tournaments and basic solo play are already included at launch. Asynchronous cups require a distinct attendance policy rather than silently changing no-wait forfeits.

## Main risks
All six games across multiple modes create substantial rule-design scope. Unlimited ties conflict with predictable duration. Daily currency plus wealth rankings can reward tenure. Uncapped stakes can make bankruptcy common. Sequential eight-player cups leave entrants waiting. These risks should be tested and discussed; they do not override the owner's decisions.
